To reach Rebirth 1: Must hit Level 10
To reach Rebirth 2: Must hit Level 20
When a player meets the required Level, the Rebirth upgrade becomes available . The player must explicitly accept it.
Upon accepting a Rebirth: The Rebirth stat increases by +1. The player's Power resets to 1, and Level resets to 1.
